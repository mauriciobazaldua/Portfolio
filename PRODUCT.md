## Users

Three distinct audiences, one site — established in [PROJECT-CONTEXT.md](./PROJECT-CONTEXT.md):

1. **HR / recruiters** — scan in seconds for a seniority signal: title match, years, recognizable companies. Not evaluating craft.
2. **Non-design hiring managers** (PM, engineering lead, exec) — skim for business impact, outcomes, and evidence of cross-functional collaboration.
3. **Designer peers** — read deeply for process rigor, decision rationale, and craft.

## Product Purpose

A portfolio for Mauricio Bazaldua, presenting four case studies from his work as a UX Designer at Xeneta, built to convert recruiter screens into interviews for Senior Product Designer roles. Success also means substantiating the *Senior* claim on its own evidence — ownership of ambiguity, cross-functional impact, and research rigor — not just asserting it.

## Positioning

Mauricio's differentiator is ownership, not just output: he runs the full arc from research through to delivery — handing off to engineering, then staying accountable for the build and tracking real usage after launch, rather than treating the job as done at handoff.

The second thread is that he builds on precedent instead of re-solving the same problem from zero each time: pulling forward findings and patterns from past projects, which is also the root of his interest in design systems — a system is what makes reuse-over-reinvention durable rather than one-off. This shows up directly in the case study evidence already on record: extending the Compass design system component for Carrier Scorecard's table/chart switcher, and escalating a systemic table-scrolling issue to the Compass team rather than patching it locally.

Both threads are corroborated by evidence already in hand, not just claimed: a documented research reversal handled correctly (Carrier Scorecard), a problem → change → revalidation loop inside a single study (Homepage onboarding), and post-acquisition product migration work (the eeSea absorption) that required deciding what to keep rather than cloning wholesale.

## Product Principles

1. **One structure, three depths.** Every case study scans in seconds, yields its business impact in under a minute, and still rewards a full read — one artifact, not three separate portfolios for three audiences.
2. **Evidence over assertion.** Every claim traces to a real quote or number already on record in the source documents. Never invent or embellish.
3. **Confidentiality before compellingness.** Anonymize named companies and soften commercial figures until Xeneta explicitly clears them — narrative force never overrides an unresolved consent question.
4. **Ownership shows in the record, not the slogan.** The portfolio should demonstrate end-to-end ownership (research → delivery → tracked outcomes) and evidence-based reuse (design systems, precedent) through what's actually documented — not through a stated claim of it.
5. **Reads as made by a person, not templated.** Explicit requirement from Mauricio: this must not present as a generic or AI-templated portfolio. Recorded here as a product principle; the actual visual execution of it is a `new-work` decision, not an init decision.
