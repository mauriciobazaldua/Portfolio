# Project Context — Senior Product Designer Portfolio

Established via `/intent` context mode. Read this before running `/strategize`, `/organize`, `/journey`, `/wireframe`, `/articulate`, or any other Intent-routed skill on this project — it's the shared understanding those skills build on.

## Users

Three distinct audiences read this portfolio differently. It has to work for all three without any one of them getting in the others' way.

1. **HR / recruiters** — Scan, don't read. Seconds per profile. Looking for: title match, years of experience, recognizable company names, a clear seniority signal. They are not evaluating craft. If they can't tell in a glance that this person is a legitimate Senior Product Designer candidate, they move on.
2. **Non-design hiring managers** (PM, engineering lead, exec) — Skim, don't scan. Looking for: business impact, outcomes/metrics, evidence of cross-functional collaboration and ownership. They care whether this person moved the needle and can work with their team — not the mechanics of the craft.
3. **Designer peers** — Read deeply. Looking for: process rigor, decision rationale, methodology, evidence of real thinking behind the work. This is the audience that will actually evaluate whether the work is good.

**Implication:** this is a progressive-disclosure problem, not a "pick one audience" problem. Each case study needs a scan layer (recruiters), an impact-summary layer (hiring managers), and a full-process layer (designer peers) — not three separate portfolios.

## Product

- **What it is:** A new personal portfolio site for a Senior Product Designer, built to support an active job search.
- **Maturity:** Pre-content. The repo is currently empty aside from installed Claude Code skills (`/intent`, `/impeccable`, and Intent's sub-skills).
- **Content status:** Raw material only — Figma files, decks — for the projects to be featured. The process narratives (research, decisions taken, outcomes) have not been written yet. Turning raw artifacts into case study stories is prerequisite work that comes before any layout or IA decisions.
- **Positioning:** Needs to substantiate a *Senior* level claim specifically — ownership of ambiguous problems, cross-functional influence, and outcomes at a scope beyond individual screens/flows — not just "designer did the work."

## Ethical stance

- Represent impact and outcomes honestly — no inflated or fabricated metrics.
- No manipulative urgency or scarcity patterns in copy (this is a portfolio, not a conversion funnel).
- Minimal or no visitor tracking; if analytics are added later, default to privacy-respecting, opt-in.
